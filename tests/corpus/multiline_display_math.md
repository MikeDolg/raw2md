# System of equations outside a table

The section reproduces the layout of the Word equation editor: a system of
equations is written with a curly brace, the opening environment sits on the
first line of the typesetting, and the closing one several lines below,
outside a table.

The strain field of the plate is set by the system:

$$\left\{ \begin{array}{r}
\varepsilon_{x} = \varepsilon_{x}(x, y) \\
\gamma_{x} = 0
\end{array} \right.$$

The solution is unique for the given boundary conditions.
