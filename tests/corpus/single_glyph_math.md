# Single glyphs in math

The section reproduces a case of the Word equation editor: the author types an
ordinary prose sign through it – a diameter, a degree, a temperature – rather
than a variable. Genuine one-letter variables of the calculation stay beside
them in the same sentences.

The pipe $\varnothing$ holds the radius $R$ and the fit diameter $D$.

The bend angle $\circ$ matches the design angle $\theta$.

The plain sign $°$ is marked next to the temperature $℃$ on the chart.
